\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\emph{Immersion} in this paper describes a property of the system: how far the display and tracking
hardware can serve the sensory channels of the body, independent of who is
wearing it~\cite{Slater.1997}. \emph{Presence} is the user's response to that
system, the sense of being in the depicted place rather than watching
it~\cite{Slater.1997, Skarbez.2017}. A passive haptic proxy is an unusual entry
on the immersion side of that ledger: it costs nothing in engineering terms and
serves a sensory channel the headset leaves empty altogether. Whether it buys presence is the empirical question we ask.
Section~\ref{sec:rw-passive-haptics} covers proxy objects and how far they may
deviate from what they stand in for; Section~\ref{sec:rw-presence-emotion}
covers presence, its measurement, and what VR does to emotion.
