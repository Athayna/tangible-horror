\subsection{Procedure}
\label{sec:study-procedure}

Sessions took place in a work room at Hochschule RheinMain that we prepared as the play area, with the proxy stations set up and calibrated before participants arrived. We told participants beforehand that they would take part in a study on VR horror, and they gave verbal consent; we used no written consent forms, a point we return to in Section~\ref{sec:discussion-limitations}.

After a short briefing on the headset, the controls, and the horror content (participants knew the genre, not the individual challenges), each participant put on the Quest~3 and played their first condition until they escaped the cabin. They then took the headset off and filled in the IPQ. The same steps repeated for the second condition, followed by the second IPQ. We disinfected the headset after each session. Between conditions the experimenters prepared the next playthrough, depending on the assigned order. One experimenter operated the water spray for the pipe challenge in the haptic condition. Timing it required no screen mirroring: the virtual burst triggers when the coin leaves the collider in front of the pipe, so the experimenter watched the participant's hand and started spraying the moment the physical coin left the physical pipe. 

A playthrough took between ten and fifteen minutes depending on how long participants searched, and a full session with both conditions and both questionnaires took about 45 minutes.
