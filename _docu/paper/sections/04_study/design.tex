\subsection{Study Design}
\label{sec:study-design}

We compared two conditions in a within-subject design. In the \emph{haptic} condition, all four physical proxies were in place and aligned with their virtual counterparts; in the \emph{baseline} condition, participants played the identical scene with no physical objects in the play area. Every participant played both conditions in a single session, completing the full cabin escape each time. The order of conditions was randomized per participant to counter order effects: roughly half started with the proxies, half without. \todoin{State the actual split once the participant log is in.}

The condition comparison is deliberately coarse: proxies are either all present or all absent. A per-challenge manipulation would have been more informative about which proxy carries the effect, but at the cost of a much larger design; with a single playthrough per condition we judged the all-or-nothing contrast to be the honest scope for a project of this size.
