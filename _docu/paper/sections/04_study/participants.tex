\subsection{Participants}
\label{sec:study-participants}

Seven participants took part (three women, four men). We recruited partly through personal invitations and partly by approaching people on campus, which produced a wide age range for a student project: the youngest participant was 18, the oldest around 60. We collected no demographic data beyond what we observed, so exact ages and prior VR experience went unrecorded. Participation was voluntary and uncompensated.

Five of the seven participants completed both study conditions; the remaining two completed only the haptic playthrough, so their data enters the descriptive statistics without joining the paired comparison.
