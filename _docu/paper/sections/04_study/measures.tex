\subsection{Measures}
\label{sec:study-measures}

We measured presence with the Igroup Presence Questionnaire (IPQ)~\cite{Schubert.2001}, completed once after each condition. The IPQ consists of 14 items on 7-point scales and yields a general presence item (``the sense of being there'') plus three subscales: spatial presence, involvement, and experienced realism. We chose the IPQ over alternatives on two grounds. First, its subscales separate the aspects a  haptic proxy should move: if the proxy works by adding a valid sensorimotor action, the gain should concentrate on spatial presence and involvement rather than on experienced realism. Second, in a systematic comparison of four presence questionnaires across five isolated presence dimensions, the IPQ was the most sensitive instrument for place illusion and the one whose score differences participants agreed with most often ~\cite{presence-questionnaires}. Since our manipulation is a place-illusion
manipulation in Slater's sense, this makes the IPQ the appropriate choice rather than a merely convenient one.
